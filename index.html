    How to perform a sensitivity analysis

    1. Pick a scenario from the Scenario list, or click New… to create one.
    2. On the Base Values tab, enter last year's results. The results show net profit, profit per partner, margin and gross hourly rate.
    3. On the People Power, Fees & Rates and Costs tabs, set targets. Type a value, drag the slider, or use the − and + buttons.
    4. Watch the Profit Impact column and the totals at the bottom. Targeted Profit updates with every change.
    5. Click Save to keep the scenario, Reset to put the targets back to base, or Print for the report.
    6. Open the Action tab for ideas on reaching the targets.

    How the numbers are worked out

    Total people = partners × (1 + leverage)
    Hours charged = total people × time available × productivity
    Net fees = hours charged × hourly rate × disbursement factor × value index × billing index
    Total costs = average cost × team members + variable cost × hours charged + fixed costs
    Net profit = net fees − total costs

    Profit impact: the targets are applied one at a time in this order: partners, leverage, time, productivity, rate, value index, billing index, average cost, variable cost, fixed costs. Each impact is the change in profit at that step, so the impacts add up to the additional profit. If you change partners and leave leverage at its base value, the number of team members stays the same, as in the original program. A target within 0.005 of its base value is treated as unchanged.
